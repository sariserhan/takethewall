## Sharing behavior

Post to X opens a composer with text and the public takeover referral link. It does not post automatically or upload the downloaded card. An owner can attach that image manually. The homepage OG image reads the real current owner, falls back to generic branding if unavailable, and permits a 60-second image cache. Social platforms may retain older previews for longer; an already-posted card is not a live billboard.

Replacement emails open a protected, prefilled draft through the existing owner login flow. The buyer still reviews and confirms checkout. A saved wallet and immediate payment are not guaranteed. Moderation removals receive a report link instead of a retake prompt.
