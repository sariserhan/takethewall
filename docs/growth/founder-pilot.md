# Seven-day founder pilot

Prepared September 14, 2026. Invitations and posts have not been sent. Recipient
names, a sending channel, and the launch date are still to be chosen. Start with
3–5 actual creators; expand to 10 only after reviewing their experience.

## Ready in the product

In `/admin`, choose **Growth → Start a small founder launch**. Enter the
recipient’s project, select the channel, and copy the draft and homepage link.
Add a specific opening based on something you tried. The copy states the $4.99
base price, replacement rule, and absence of guaranteed duration or audience.
It does not promise a prize, earnings, or traffic.

Download the results sheet there, or use
[the CSV template](../../public/launch/founder-pilot-results.csv). Each row is one
UTC day's observations. Recipient emails and private owner links belong in
private records, never this repository or the public CSV.

## Daily actions

| Day | Action | Evidence to record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Choose 3–5 relevant creators and record a short real preview demonstration. Capture the Funnel baseline. | Selected public project URLs in private notes; baseline counts. |
| 2 | Send personalized invitations through the agreed channel. | Actual number sent, channel and campaign link. |
| 3 | Watch a willing participant try the site. Ask what they think they are buying and where they hesitate. | Exact feedback; checkout errors, if any. |
| 4 | Fix the most common obstacle. Ask participants who replied whether the change helps. | One change and the evidence behind it. |
| 5 | Invite up to five more relevant creators. Offer the demo to people who ask for it. | Replies and measured traffic. |
| 6 | With permission, share one real owner's public takeover page and design. | Public post URL; use the owner's own referral link when crediting their traffic. |
| 7 | Review results and decide whether to broaden outreach or fix the experience first. | Actual totals, recurring objections, next action. |

Use the existing [first owners kit](first-owners-kit.md) for the video storyboard
and [channel drafts](launch-channel-drafts.md) for a later public announcement.
Do not pick a public launch date until the production purchase flow is verified.

## Measurement

Campaign: `founder_pilot`. The generated `utm_source` distinguishes personal,
X, and LinkedIn links. All lead directly to the homepage. Leave a metric blank
when the provider does not report it; blank does not mean zero.

- Channel visits: use the analytics provider's available campaign report.
- Site activity: **Admin → Funnel** provides measured visits, checkout sessions,
  and confirmed paid activations. It excludes admin publications, demo additions,
  and test payments. Record the same UTC window each time.
- The Funnel's last-seven-day totals are rolling, not daily values. Do not add
  those snapshots together. For a daily row, use the difference between two
  snapshots only if their start date is identical; otherwise retain the full
  window dates in the feedback/notes column and compare like periods.
- Channel traffic and site-wide purchases are separate. A UTM link does not prove
  that an invitation produced a purchase, and these ratios are not matched-user
  conversion rates.
- Owner feedback is in **Admin → Growth → Owner feedback**. Checkout complaints
  should be investigated individually, not inferred from a small sample.

If few people visit, revise the invitation and demonstration. If people visit
but cannot explain the offer, revise the first screen. If they start checkout
but fail to publish, inspect the payment flow before inviting more people. Report
zero purchases honestly; this pilot is for learning, not a promised sales target.
